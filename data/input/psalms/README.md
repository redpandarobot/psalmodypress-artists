# Psalms Input Directory

## Instructions

Place your 150 psalm text files here using the following naming convention:

```
psalm_001.txt
psalm_002.txt
psalm_003.txt
...
psalm_150.txt
```

## File Format

- **Format**: Plain text (.txt)
- **Naming**: Zero-padded numbers (001-150)
- **Content**: The psalm text from your chosen translation (e.g., WEB Translation)

## Example File: psalm_001.txt

```
Blessed is the man who doesn't walk in the counsel of the wicked,
nor stand on the path of sinners,
nor sit in the seat of scoffers;
but his delight is in Yahweh's law.
On his law he meditates day and night.
He will be like a tree planted by the streams of water,
that produces its fruit in its season,
whose leaf also does not wither.
Whatever he does shall prosper.
The wicked are not so,
but are like the chaff which the wind drives away.
Therefore the wicked shall not stand in the judgment,
nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.
For Yahweh knows the way of the righteous,
but the way of the wicked shall perish.
```

## Notes

- Keep the original line breaks and formatting from your translation
- Each file should contain only the psalm text (no titles or headers needed)
- Encoding: UTF-8
