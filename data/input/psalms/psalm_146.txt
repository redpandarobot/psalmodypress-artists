Praise the LORD
Praise the LORD, my soul
While I live, I will praise the LORD
I will sing praises to my God as long as I exist
Dont put your trust in princes, in a son of man in whom there is no help
His spirit departs, and he returns to the earth
In that very day, his thoughts perish
Happy is he who has the God of Jacob for his help, whose hope is in the LORD, his God
Who made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that is in them
Who keeps truth forever
Who executes justice for the oppressed; who gives food to the hungry
The LORD frees the prisoners
The LORD opens the eyes of the blind
The LORD raises up those who are bowed down
The LORD loves the righteous
The LORD preserves the foreigners
He upholds the fatherless and widow
But he turns the way of the wicked upside down
The LORD will reign forever; your God, O Zion, to all generations
Praise the LORD
