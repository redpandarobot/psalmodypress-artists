A Psalm by the sons of Korah; a Song
His foundation is in the holy mountains
The LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob
Glorious things are spoken about you, city of God
Selah
I will record Rahab and Babylon amongst those who acknowledge me
Behold, Philistia, Tyre, and also Ethiopia: This one was born there
Yes, of Zion it will be said, This one and that one was born in her
The Most High himself will establish her
The LORD will count, when he writes up the peoples, This one was born there
Selah
Those who sing as well as those who dance say, All my springs are in you
