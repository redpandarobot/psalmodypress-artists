A Song of Ascents
By David
See how good and how pleasant it is for brothers to live together in unity
It is like the precious oil on the head that ran down on the beard
Even Aarons beard that came down on the edge of his robes
Like the dew of Hermon, that comes down on the hills of Zion
For there the LORD gives the blessing, even life forever more
