[Verse 1]
Praise the LORD
Blessed is the man who fears the LORD, who delights greatly in his commandments

[Chorus]
Praise the LORD

[Verse 3]
He will maintain his cause in judgement
For he will never be shaken
The righteous will be remembered forever
