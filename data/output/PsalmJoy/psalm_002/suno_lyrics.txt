Title: Psalm 2 - Why do the nations rage, and [00005]
Sequence ID: 00005
Psalm: 2
Artist: PsalmJoy

============================================================

[Verse 1]
Why do the nations rage, and the peoples plot a vain thing
The kings of the earth take a stand
And the rulers take counsel together, against the LORD
And

[Chorus]
Why do the nations rage, and the peoples plot a vain thing

[Verse 2]
Against his Anointed, saying
Lets break their bonds apart, and cast their cords from us
He who sits in the heavens will laugh
The Lord will have them in derision

[Chorus]
Why do the nations rage, and the peoples plot a vain thing

[Verse 3]
Then he will speak to them in his anger, and terrify them in his wrath:
Yet I have set my King on my holy hill of Zion
I will tell of the decree: The LORD said to me, You are my son
Today I have become your father
