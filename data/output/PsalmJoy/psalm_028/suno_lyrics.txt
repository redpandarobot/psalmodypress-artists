[Verse 1]
By David
To you, LORD, I call
My rock dont be deaf to me lest if you are silent to me
I would become like those who go down into the pit

[Chorus]
By David

[Verse 2]
Hear the voice of my petitions when I cry to you
When I lift up my hands towards your Most Holy Place
Dont draw me away with the wicked
With the workers of iniquity who speak peace with their neighbours

[Chorus]
By David

[Verse 3]
But mischief is in their hearts
Give them according to their work
And according to the wickedness of their doings
Give them according to the operation of their hands
