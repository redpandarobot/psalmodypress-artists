Title: Psalm 104 - Bless the LORD, my soul [000BH]
Sequence ID: 000BH
Psalm: 104
Artist: PsalmJoy

============================================================

[Verse 1]
Bless the LORD, my soul
The LORD, my God, you are very great
You are clothed with honour and majesty
He covers himself with light as with a garment

[Chorus]
Bless the LORD, my soul

[Verse 2]
He stretches out the heavens like a curtain
He lays the beams of his rooms in the waters
He makes the clouds his chariot
He walks on the wings of the wind

[Chorus]
Bless the LORD, my soul

[Verse 3]
He makes his messengers winds, and his servants flames of fire
He laid the foundations of the earth, that it should not be moved forever
You covered it with the deep as with a cloak
The waters stood above the mountains
