[Verse 1]
A Psalm by David
Ascribe to the LORD, you sons of the mighty, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength
Ascribe to the LORD the glory due to his name
Worship the LORD in holy array

[Chorus]
A Psalm by David

[Verse 2]
The LORDs voice is on the waters
The God of glory thunders, even the LORD on many waters
The LORDs voice is powerful
The LORDs voice is full of majesty

[Chorus]
A Psalm by David

[Verse 3]
The LORDs voice breaks the cedars
Yes, the LORD breaks in pieces the cedars of Lebanon
He makes them also to skip like a calf; Lebanon and Sirion like a young, wild ox
The LORDs voice strikes with flashes of lightning
