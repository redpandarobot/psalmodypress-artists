Title: Psalm 42 - For the Chief Musician [0004L]
Sequence ID: 0004L
Psalm: 42
Artist: PsalmJoy

============================================================

[Verse 1]
For the Chief Musician
A contemplation by the sons of Korah
As the deer pants for the water brooks, so my soul pants after you, God
My soul thirsts for God, for the living God

[Chorus]
For the Chief Musician

[Verse 2]
When shall I come and appear before God
My tears have been my food day and night, while they continually ask me, Where is your God
These things I remember
And pour out my soul within me, how I used to go with the crowd

[Chorus]
For the Chief Musician

[Verse 3]
And
Led them to Gods house, with the voice of joy and praise, a multitude keeping a holy day
Why are you in despair, my soul
Why are you disturbed within me
