Title: Psalm 102 - A Prayer of the afflicted, when [000B9]
Sequence ID: 000B9
Psalm: 102
Artist: PsalmJoy

============================================================

[Verse 1]
A Prayer of the afflicted, when he is overwhelmed and pours out his complaint before the LORD
Hear my prayer, LORD
Let my cry come to you
Dont hide your face from me in the day of my distress

[Chorus]
A Prayer of the afflicted, when he is overwhelmed and pours out his complaint before the LORD

[Verse 2]
Turn your ear to me
Answer me quickly in the day when I call
For my days consume away like smoke
My bones are burnt as a torch

[Chorus]
A Prayer of the afflicted, when he is overwhelmed and pours out his complaint before the LORD

[Verse 3]
My heart is blighted like grass, and withered, for I forget to eat my bread
By reason of the voice of my groaning, my bones stick to my skin
I am like a pelican of the wilderness
I have become as an owl of the waste places
