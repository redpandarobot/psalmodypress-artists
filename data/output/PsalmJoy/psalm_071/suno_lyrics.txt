Title: Psalm 71 - In you, LORD, I take refuge [0007T]
Sequence ID: 0007T
Psalm: 71
Artist: PsalmJoy

============================================================

[Verse 1]
In you, LORD, I take refuge
Never let me be disappointed
Deliver me in your righteousness, and rescue me
Turn your ear to me, and save me

[Chorus]
In you, LORD, I take refuge

[Verse 2]
Be to me a rock of refuge to which I may always go
Give the command to save me, for you are my rock and my fortress
Rescue me, my God, from the hand of the wicked, from the hand of the unrighteous and cruel man
For you are my hope, Lord GOD, my confidence from my youth

[Chorus]
In you, LORD, I take refuge

[Verse 3]
I have relied on you from the womb
You are he who took me out of my mothers womb
I will always praise you
I am a marvel to many, but you are my strong refuge
