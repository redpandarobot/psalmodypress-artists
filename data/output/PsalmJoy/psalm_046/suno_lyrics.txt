[Verse 1]
For the Chief Musician
By the sons of Korah
According to Alamoth
God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble

[Chorus]
For the Chief Musician

[Verse 2]
Therefore we wont be afraid though the earth changes
Though the mountains are shaken into the heart of the seas
Though its waters roar and are troubled, though the mountains tremble with their swelling
Selah

[Chorus]
For the Chief Musician

[Verse 3]
There is a river the streams of which make the city of God glad
The holy place of the tents of the Most High
God is within her
She shall not be moved
