[Verse 1]
By David
Judge me, LORD, for I have walked in my integrity
I have trusted also in the LORD without wavering
Examine me, LORD, and prove me

[Chorus]
By David

[Verse 2]
Try my heart and my mind
For your loving kindness is before my eyes
I have walked in your truth
I have not sat with deceitful men, neither will I go in with hypocrites

[Chorus]
By David

[Verse 3]
I hate the assembly of evildoers, and will not sit with the wicked
I will wash my hands in innocence, so I will go about your altar, LORD
That I may make the voice of thanksgiving to be heard and tell of all your wondrous deeds
LORD, I love the habitation of your house, the place where your glory dwells
